### Oven-roasted chicken fillet
*with broccolini and caramelized onion sauce*

- **Total time:** 30 minutes
- **Calories:** 520 kcal
- **Protein:** 45.5g
- **Fiber:** 36.9g
- **Tags:** Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Child-friendly, More green, Protein-rich, Under 650 calories, Source of fiber

**Ingredients (2 servings)**

Included in delivery:
- Red wine sauce (Contains: Sulfites) — 60g
- Potato — 500g
- Balsamic vinegar (Contains: Sulfites) — 12 ml
- Onion — 1 pc
- Thyme — 1 pc
- Chicken breast fillet — 300g
- Broccoli (broccolini) — 200g
- Garlic and herb seasoning — 4g

Not included (pantry staples):
- Milk for mash — 3 tbsp
- Salt for mash — 1/2 tsp
- Salt for meat — 1/4 tsp
- Oil for frying — 1 tbsp
- Sugar for caramelized onions — 1/2 tsp
- Water for caramelized onions — 2 tbsp
- Water for sauce — 1/2 cup

**Steps**
1. **Make mashed potatoes** — Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C (fan). Bring a medium-sized saucepan of salted water to a boil. Peel and dice potatoes into 2 cm pieces. Boil potatoes for 11–13 minutes, or until they can be easily split with a knife. Pour off the water, and mash the potatoes with milk, salt and a pinch of pepper in the saucepan. Set aside under a lid. TIP: Add a knob of butter to make the mash extra creamy.
2. **Roast chicken** — Season chicken with garlic and herb seasoning, salt and a pinch of pepper. Heat olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Fry chicken for 2 minutes per side, or until browned. IMPORTANT: Wash hands and utensils thoroughly after handling raw chicken.
3. **Roast broccolini and chicken** — Place the broccolini on one half of a baking sheet lined with parchment paper. Toss with a drizzle of olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper. Place chicken on the other half of the baking sheet. Cook broccolini and chicken in the oven for 10–12 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through. TIPS: The chicken is done when the meat is no longer pink in the middle; use multiple baking sheets if it gets too crowded.
4. **Caramelize onions** — Meanwhile, cut the onion into thin slices. Pick thyme leaves. Heat the same frying pan (do not wipe it dry) over medium heat. Add a drizzle of olive oil if necessary. Fry onion for 5–6 minutes, until soft. Add balsamic vinegar, thyme and sugar. Stir and simmer for 2–3 minutes, or until caramelized. TIP: Hold the top of the thyme stalk and pull your fingers along the stem to release the leaves.
5. **Cook onion sauce** — Reduce heat to medium and add red wine sauce and water to the skillet. Simmer for 3–4 minutes, or until slightly thickened. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper.
6. **Serve** — Place mashed potatoes, broccolini and chicken on plates. Spoon over caramelized onion sauce.

### Asian shrimp tacos
*with pico de gallo and bang bang sauce*

- **Total time:** 20 minutes
- **Calories:** 639 kcal
- **Protein:** 28.5g
- **Fiber:** 15.8g
- **Tags:** Fast, Fix me first, More green, Under 650 calories, Source of fiber

**Ingredients (2 servings)**

Included in delivery:
- Coriander — 1 pc
- Around the World (spice blend, Contains: Eggs, Mustard) — 2g
- Citrus fruit — 1 pc
- Tomato — 1 pc
- Cucumber — 1/2 pc
- King prawns (Contains: Crustaceans) — 240g
- Vegan Sriracha Mayonnaise — 50g
- Red cabbage, pre-cut — 75g
- Onion — 1 pc
- Sweet chili sauce — 40g
- Salad — 1/2 pc
- Mini tortillas, whole grain (Contains: Gluten, Wheat) — 8 pcs

Not included (pantry staples):
- Salt for salsa — 1/4 tsp
- Salt for shrimp — 1/4 tsp
- Salt for sauce — 1/4 tsp

**Steps**
1. **Prepare fruit & vegetables** — Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C (fan) if you don't have a microwave. Finely chop cucumber, tomato and onion. Prepare the salad and separate the lettuce leaves. Pick coriander leaves. Cut citrus fruit into wedges.
2. **Mix the pico de gallo** — Mix tomato, cucumber, onion, cilantro, a generous squeeze of citrus juice, salt and a pinch of pepper in a medium bowl.
3. **Fry shrimp** — Coarsely chop the shrimp on a separate cutting board and season with salt. Heat a splash of olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. 6. **Serve** — Fill tortillas with lettuce, red cabbage, pico de gallo and shrimp. Squeeze over citrus juice and drizzle generously with bang bang sauce.

### Cowboy chili with chicken
*One version for children, one for adults*

- **Total time:** 30 minutes
- **Calories:** 774 kcal
- **Protein:** 47.5g
- **Fiber:** 9.2g
- **Tags:** Gluten-free, Dairy-free, Child-friendly, Comfort Food

**Ingredients (2 servings)**

Included in delivery:
- Chicken breast fillet — 300g
- Maize (corn) — 150g
- Crushed tomatoes — 390g
- Chili — 1 pc
- Tex-Mex (spice blend) — 5g
- Basmati rice — 150g
- Onion — 1 pc
- Broth — 4g
- Carrot, Julienne — 1 pc
- Ay Cajun-ba (Contains: Sulfites) — 4g
- Chimichurri — 30g

Not included (pantry staples):
- Water for rice — 3 dl
- Salt for rice — 1/2 tsp
- Salt for meat — 1/4 tsp
- Water for sauce — 2 dl
- Sugar for sauce — 1 tsp
- Salt for sauce — 1/2 tsp
- Oil for chimichurri — 2 tbsp

**Steps**
1. **Boil rice** — Bring a medium-sized saucepan of water to a boil. Stir in rice and salt, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, covered, for 10 minutes. Remove the saucepan from the heat and let rest, covered, for 10 minutes. TIP: The rice should be steaming, so don't peek!
2. **Prepare vegetables** — Meanwhile, finely chop the onion and chili. Drain the corn.
3. **Roast chicken** — Heat a splash of olive oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add chicken and season with salt. Fry for 2 minutes per side, or until browned. Transfer to a plate and set aside. IMPORTANT: Wash hands and utensils thoroughly after handling raw chicken.

5. **Mix chimichurri** — Meanwhile, mix chimichurri, chili, olive oil, and a pinch of salt and pepper in a small bowl. Remove cooked chicken from the pan and place in a large bowl. Shred chicken into pieces using 2 forks. TIP: Skip the chili or use less if you don't like spicy food.
6. **Serve** — Place rice and chili stew in deep plates and top with shredded chicken. Drizzle chimichurri over the adult portions.

### Vietnamese shrimp
*with garlic rice and pickled carrot*

- **Total time:** 25 minutes
- **Calories:** 739 kcal
- **Protein:** 27.3g
- **Fiber:** 7.1g
- **Tags:** Fast, Child-friendly, Fix me first, Comfort Food

**Ingredients (2 servings)**

Included in delivery:
- Long grain white rice — 150g
- Citrus fruit — 1 pc
- Pak choi — 1 pc
- Salted peanuts (Contains: Peanuts) — 20g
- Soy sauce (Contains: Wheat, Soy, Gluten) — 20 ml
- Chili — 1/2 pc
- Garlic clove — 1 pc
- King prawns (Contains: Crustaceans) — 240g
- White wine vinegar (Contains: Sulfites) — 12 ml
- Vegan Sriracha Mayonnaise — 50g
- Carrot — 1 pc
- Onion — 1 pc
- Coriander — 1 pc

Not included (pantry staples):
- Butter for rice — 1/2 tbsp
- Water for rice — 3 dl
- Salt for rice — 1/2 tsp
- Sugar for pickling — 1/2 tsp
- Water for pickling — 1 tbsp
- Flour for meat — 2 tbsp
- Salt for shrimp — 1/4 tsp
- Sugar for marinade — 2 tsp
- Oil for frying — 1/2 tbsp

**Steps**
1. **Cook garlic rice** — Finely chop or press the garlic. Heat butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Sauté half of the garlic for 30 seconds, or until fragrant. Add water and bring to a boil. Stir in rice and salt, reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, covered, for 14 minutes. Remove the saucepan from the heat and let rest, covered, for 10 minutes. TIP: The rice should be steaming, so don't peek!
2. **Prepare vegetables** — Meanwhile, peel the carrot into long strips with a potato peeler and finely chop the last innermost piece. Coarsely chop the pak choi. Coarsely chop the onion. Cut citrus fruit into wedges.
3. **Pickled carrot** — Mix white wine vinegar, sugar, water and a pinch of salt in a small bowl. Turn carrot in the mixture and set aside. Pour off the mixture before serving. Mix wheat flour, shrimp, salt and a pinch of pepper in a large bowl. Mix soy sauce, sugar, a generous splash of citrus juice and the remaining garlic in a small bowl.
4. **Fry shrimp** — Heat olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Sauté pak choi for 2–3 minutes, or until tender. Season with a pinch of salt and pepper and transfer to a bowl with a lid. Heat the same frying pan over medium heat, adding a drizzle of olive oil if needed. Fry the shrimp with the onion for 1–2 minutes per side, or until golden and cooked through. Remove the frying pan from the heat and pour the marinade over the shrimp to coat. TIP: The shrimp are done when they are pink on the outside and opaque in the middle.
5. **Prepare ingredients** — Meanwhile, coarsely chop peanuts. Coarsely chop the coriander. Halve, core and cut the chili into thin slices.
6. **Serve** — Place garlic rice, shrimp and vegetables in bowls. Top with peanuts and garnish with coriander and chili. Serve with a dollop of sriracha mayonnaise and citrus fruit wedges to squeeze over the dish. TIP: Skip the chili or use less if you don't like spicy food.

### BBQ-spiced pulled chicken
*with Cajun roasted vegetables and citrus dressing*

- **Total time:** 30 minutes
- **Calories:** 577 kcal
- **Protein:** 42.3g
- **Fiber:** 10.6g
- **Tags:** Source of fiber
- **Description:** Tonight we're making our own pulled chicken from BBQ-seasoned chicken. The chicken is accompanied in the oven by Cajun-seasoned peppers, onions and broccolini. At the same time, we're cooking bulgur, which we then mix with fresh spinach. We top the delights with a delicious citrus dressing.

**Ingredients (2 servings)**

Included in delivery:
- Citrus fruit — 1 pc
- Sour cream (Contains: Milk) — 75 ml
- Spinach — 30g
- BBQ sauce (Contains: Mustard) — 30g
- Chicken breast fillet, BBQ marinated — 300g
- Ay Cajun-ba (Contains: Sulfites) — 4g
- Bulgur (Contains: Wheat, Gluten) — 85g
- Onion — 2 pcs
- Pepper — 1 pc
- Broccolini — 100g

Not included (pantry staples):
- Salt for meat — 1/4 tsp
- Salt for vegetables — 1/4 tsp
- Water for bulgur — 2.5 dl
- Salt for bulgur — 1/2 tsp
- Salt for dressing — 1/4 tsp
- Oil for salad — 1 tbsp
- Salt for salad — 1/4 tsp

**Steps**
1. **Roast chicken and vegetables** — Preheat the oven to 220°C/200°C (fan). Place the chicken on one half of a baking sheet lined with baking paper and season with salt. Dice the peppers into 2 cm pieces. Cut the onion into 2 cm wide wedges. Place vegetables on the other half of the baking sheet. Toss with a drizzle of olive oil, Ay Cajun-ba, salt and a pinch of pepper. Roast in the oven for 15–25 minutes, until vegetables are soft and chicken is cooked through. IMPORTANT: Wash hands and utensils thoroughly after handling raw chicken. TIP: The chicken is ready when the meat is no longer pink in the middle.
2. **Boil bulgur** — Bring a medium-sized saucepan of water to a boil. Add bulgur and salt and reduce heat to medium-low. Simmer, covered, for 9–10 minutes, or until "al dente".
3. **Make citrus dressing** — Meanwhile, coarsely chop the broccolini. When there are 12 minutes left in the cooking time for the chicken and vegetables, add the broccolini to the tray and roast in the oven until done. Cut citrus fruit into wedges. Mix sour cream and a splash of citrus juice in a small bowl. Season with salt and a pinch of pepper.
4. **Add spinach** — Rinse the spinach. Stir the spinach, olive oil and salt into the saucepan with the finished bulgur.
5. **Make pulled chicken** — Use two forks to shred until the chicken is completely shredded. Mix shredded chicken, BBQ sauce and a splash of citrus juice in a bowl until the chicken is covered with sauce.
6. **Serve** — Place spinach bulgur in bowls and top with BBQ-seasoned pulled chicken, roasted vegetables and citrus dressing.
